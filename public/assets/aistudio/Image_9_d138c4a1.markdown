## Brand & Style

This design system drives an AI-first travel intelligence platform built to inspire, curate, and orchestrate global discovery. It pairs the electric clarity of advanced machine intelligence with the warm, tactile sensory anticipation of international transit. 

The aesthetic is **Vibrant Modernism with Frosted Tactility**—an interplay of high-energy chromatics, deep atmospheric gradients, airy whitespace, and soft glassmorphic layering. The interface avoids cold, clinical algorithmic conventions; instead, it uses luminous cobalt structures, sunny mandarin highlights, and refreshing mint verification states against crisp, floating surfaces to evoke optimism, boundless momentum, and wanderlust.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Contemporary wanderers, remote professionals, digital nomads, and spontaneous adventurers who demand algorithmic precision paired with editorial elegance.
- **Atmosphere:** Energetic, effortlessly smart, sun-drenched, and frictionless.
- **Visual Tenets:**
  - *Luminous Velocity:* High-chroma accents direct the eye instantly toward discovery vectors and generated itineraries.
  - *Atmospheric Depth:* Multi-layered translucent panes simulate looking through clear observation windows onto vibrant destinations.
  - *Tactile Precision:* Softly rounded geometries balanced by structural, high-legibility geometric typography.

## Colors

The palette balances algorithmic authority, sunset vibrancy, and clear natural elements. The primary canvas rests on a warm sandy cream base (`#FAF8F5`), preventing optical fatigue while letting crisp pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) pop with structural clarity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Electric Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Symbolizes the algorithmic engine, forward flight paths, and reliable navigational structure. Used for active navigation anchors, primary action buttons, key metrics, and focus rings.
- **Secondary — Mandarin Coral (`#FF6B4A`):** The spark of travel emotion—sunsets, alerts, real-time deals, and high-impact calls to adventure.
- **Tertiary — Mint Teal (`#0D9488` with `#14B8A6` highlights):** Represents tropical waters, verified sustainability credentials, booking confirmations, and budget efficiencies.
- **Neutral Core — Sandy Cream (`#FAF8F5`) & Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas environments. Text uses deep slate navy (`#0F172A`) for absolute contrast, with subdued labels leaning on warm slate (`#64748B`).

### Functional Gradients
- **Discovery Aura:** Linear blend from `#2563EB` (0%) through `#7C3AED` (50%) to `#FF6B4A` (100%), reserved for AI itinerary generation highlights and dynamic search triggers.
- **Sunset Horizon:** Soft radial burst of `#FF6B4A` (12% opacity) bleeding into `#FAF8F5` (0%) to warm hero headers and destination portals.

## Typography

The typographic pairing unites the contemporary, geometric punch of **Outfit** for editorial titles and wayfinding markers with the humanist clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for complex itinerary tables, chat dialogs, and travel logistics.

### Structural Rules
- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Set with negative tracking to create a tight, posters-and-magazines aesthetic that feels confident and fast. Always set title text in dark slate (`#0F172A`) or reverse against image backdrops with subtle drop-shadow shields.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Calibrated for exceptional micro-reading comfort across variable flight schedules, booking matrices, and conversational AI generation bubbles.
- **Labels & Tags:** Capitalize selectively. Pill tags use `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight letter-spacing (`0.02em`–`0.04em`) to maintain legibility when overlaying photography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model runs on a flexible 12-column grid for desktop viewports, consolidating to a 6-column architecture on tablet, and a single-column stacking flow on mobile. 

### Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-container width capped at `1440px`. Full 12-column grid with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer frame margins at `margin: 2.5rem`. Search bars and itinerary planners float dynamically above hero sections.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 6-column fluid structure. Sidebars convert to collapsible bottom drawers or off-canvas sheets. Gutter adjusts to `1.25rem`, canvas margin to `1.75rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Outer canvas gutters compress to `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Complex trip views convert to swipeable carousels with peek margins of `16px`.

### Spacing Cadence
- Micro-units (`space-xs: 4px`, `space-sm: 8px`) govern icon-to-label bindings and inside chip paddings.
- Standard flow (`space-md: 16px`, `space-lg: 24px`) shapes form fields, inner card modules, and card grids.
- Macro intervals (`space-xl: 40px`) separate itinerary days, destination clusters, and dashboard panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent frosted layers, chromatic ambient glows, and crisp white surfaces resting over the warm sandy cream canvas.

### The Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Sandy Cream (`#FAF8F5`). Flat, non-interactive floor containing foundational grid structures.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces):** Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and a multi-stage ambient drop shadow:
  - *Shadow:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Modals):** Lifted interaction cards:
  - *Shadow:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating HUD & Search Console):** Glassmorphic surfaces that allow vibrant content underneath to subtly bleed through.
  - *Background:* `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`.
  - *Backdrop Filter:* `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
  - *Border:* `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`.
  - *Shadow:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (AI Spark Glow):** Dedicated to AI prompts and recommendations:
  - Ambient halo using `0 0 24px 0 rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.25)` or `0 0 24px 0 rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an open, friendly, high-aerodynamic aesthetic using rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px` on regular cards, `24px` on destination hero containers).

### Radius Map
- **Sub-elements (Inputs, Selectors, nested blocks):** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Core Cards & Panels:** `1rem` to `1.25rem` (16px – 20px).
- **Feature Modules & Modals:** `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Interactive Badges, Filters & Call-to-Actions:** Full pill style (`9999px`) to mirror physical luggage tags and travel tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Electric Flight):** Solid Cobalt (`#2563EB`) fill, text in Pure White, `border-radius: 9999px`, inner padding `12px 24px`. Hover state triggers a subtle vertical translation (`-1px`) and brightens background to `#1D4ED8` with a tailored electric glow.
- **Secondary (Wanderlust Spark):** Solid Coral (`#FF6B4A`) fill, pure white text, pill radius. Used strictly for conversions, instantaneous bookings, and primary AI triggers.
- **Ghost / Glass:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`, border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, backdrop blur `12px`. Slate dark text (`#0F172A`).

### Chips & Badges
- **AI Recommendation Badge:** Pill-shaped, gradient border from `#2563EB` to `#FF6B4A`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, displaying an AI spark icon accompanied by `label-sm` text.
- **Status Pills:** 
  - *Eco / Verified:* Mint background (`#CCFBF1`), Mint Teal text (`#0F766E`).
  - *Deal / Hot:* Coral tint (`#FFEDD5`), Coral text (`#C2410C`).
  - *Neutral Metric:* Pure white surface, subtle slate border, slate text.

### Cards & Layout Units
- **Destination Card:** Outer radius `1.25rem` (20px). Edge-to-edge photography masked by top rounded corners, with floating glassmorphic pills for ratings and price tags anchored in upper corners. Bottom section features title (`headline-sm`), brief description (`body-md`), and inline micro-tags.
- **AI Itinerary Tile:** Modular layout with left-hand chronological trail. Uses white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle cobalt line connector (`2px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`).

### Input Fields & Search Shell
- **Floating Omni-Search Bar:** Ultra-elevated glass pill console housing segmented zones for "Where to?", "Dates", and "Vibe/AI Prompt". Sub-dividers use vertical hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Standard Inputs:** Base height `48px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, radius `12px`. Focus shifts border to Electric Cobalt (`#2563EB`) with a `3px` diffuse focus ring (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Custom curved checkboxes (`radius: 6px`) and circular radio controls. Active state fills with Electric Cobalt containing an animated SVG white checkmark. Inactive state rests on an unselected border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)`.
